The week small AI models got really good

You don’t need a giant server anymore. I tried it on my own laptop.

This week three companies released small AI models. Small means they fit on a normal laptop. No internet, no monthly bill. I spent my hour today testing one on my Dell.

The setup took ten minutes. The first answer took four seconds. That’s slow next to ChatGPT, but it’s running on my machine, in my hostel room, for free.

So what is it good at? Short answers, summaries and simple code. I gave it a Python bug from ProntoPy and it found the problem on the first try. For long essays it starts to drift.

“The best AI is the one you can actually run.”my note to self, day 12

Why does this matter? Because students like me can now build AI features without paying for an API. That changes what one person can ship.

3 things to remember

  1. Small models now run on normal laptops.
  2. Good for short tasks and code. Weak on long writing.
  3. Free and private, because nothing leaves your machine.
Sources: the model’s release notes · my own test
